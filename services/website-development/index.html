Frequently asked

What’s the difference between the two packages?

Business Starter is a single page, built to get a real business online fast. Custom Website Build covers up to 5 pages and is meant for a business that needs more than one page to tell its story, like a services page, an about page, or a gallery.

Can I start with one page and add more later?

Yes. Extra pages can be added to either package at any point, at 50 BZD per page.

Do I own the site?

Yes, outright. It’s built for your business, not leased to you.

What’s included in the price?

Design and development around your business, a working contact form, and a site that loads fast and works on every screen. Hosting is separate and billed at 500 BZD/year, see the full pricing breakdown on the Build page.
