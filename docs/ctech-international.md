## Pricing & Package Strategy (finalized structure, July 2026)

**Two paths, shown side by side in the "Build Your Site" configurator (anchoring effect):**

### 1. Starter (subscription / "rental" model)
- **Price:** 99 BZD/month + 100 BZD/year domain (billed separately)
- **Single tier only** — deliberate choice to make small businesses feel personally considered rather than upsold through a tier ladder
- **Billing:** month-to-month, no annual contract (higher churn risk, offset by domain control — see retention mechanic below)
- **Domain control:** Ctech registers and controls the domain (true rental). Client does not get registrar access.
- **Restrictions (draft, adjust as needed):**
  - Up to ~5 pages (Home, About, Services/Menu, Gallery, Contact)
  - Built from Ctech's own template set, not fully custom design
  - No e-commerce, no online booking/scheduling
  - Basic on-page SEO only (titles, meta, sitemap, mobile optimization) — no ongoing SEO strategy
  - Contact form only, no CRM/automation integration
  - 1–2 small content edits/month included; bigger changes = extra fee
- **Upgrade path:** explicitly stated in the package copy — if a Starter client outgrows the plan (needs e-commerce, more pages, etc.), they move to the Custom Build. No credit for past subscription payments.
- **Retention mechanic (honest framing, not deceptive):** because Ctech controls the domain, leaving means starting a new domain from zero — which also honestly resets SEO progress built over time. This is a true, defensible reason to stay, not just lock-in.
- **Note:** needs a short written terms doc covering what happens on cancellation (grace period, takedown) to protect against disputes. Not necessarily the headline marketing message, but should exist in writing.

- **Price:** 700 BZD one-time build + 500 BZD/year hosting
- Full ownership, fully custom design, no page/feature restrictions
